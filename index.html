Who We Are

Toolan InsurTech Solutions (also known as Toolan Digital Insurance Co) is a Nigerian fintech company transforming how insurance works. Founded by an experienced API Banking Engineer, we understand the technical infrastructure needed to deliver seamless financial services.

We bridge the gap between traditional insurance and modern digital expectations. Our platform integrates with existing banking systems, payment gateways, and business software to provide insurance solutions that work the way Nigerian businesses operate.

Our expertise in API banking engineering means we build secure, scalable, and reliable insurance technology that protects what matters most to you and your business.

Digital Insurance Products & Services

API Insurance Integration

Seamless integration of insurance products into your existing platforms. Our robust APIs allow banks, fintechs, and businesses to offer insurance without building infrastructure from scratch.

Business Insurance Solutions

Comprehensive coverage for Nigerian SMEs including liability, property, and business interruption insurance. Quick quotes, instant policy issuance, and digital documentation.
