About Us

WM-Logic Ventures is a leading manufacturing company based in the heart of Alimosho, Lagos State. From our state-of-the-art facility at 25, Agric Road Egan, Fashola Bus Stop, Igando-Ikotun, we serve businesses across Nigeria with top-tier manufactured products and components.

Our commitment to precision, quality control, and timely delivery has made us the first choice for companies seeking reliable manufacturing solutions. We combine modern manufacturing techniques with deep understanding of Nigerian market needs to deliver products that meet international standards while remaining accessible to local businesses.
